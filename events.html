Upcoming Events & Experiences at Cha & Bao

  • Xiao Long Bao Folding Masterclass

    Monthly Saturday morning hands-on workshop teaching guests how to roll dough, pleat, and soup-fill authentic Xiao Long Bao.

  • Friday Dumplings & Live Jazz Nights

    Every Friday evening at 7 PM, enjoy live acoustic jazz music while sharing steamer baskets and craft cocktails.

  • Tea & Dim Sum Pairing Circle

    Join our tasting circle every first Monday evening. Learn how to pair premium loose-leaf Chinese teas with savory bao.

  • Seasonal Chef’s Tasting Flights

    Sample limited-edition micro-batches like Truffle Xiao Long Bao and Wagyu Shao Mai guided by our head chef.

  • Community Open Mic & Storytelling

    A monthly showcase for local performers, poets, and storytellers. Free order of Sweet Custard Buns for all performers.
